Compare loans in Singapore, without the runaround.

LoanWhale brings personal and home loan offers into one clean view, so you can weigh interest rates, tenures and eligibility side by side, apply in the app, and follow your application from Submitted to Approved.

LoanWhale is a demo application. It is a portfolio project that demonstrates a loan-comparison experience. It is not a bank or licensed lender and does not send applications to any financial institution.

Compare

Browse loan offers and filter by loan type to see the details that matter.

Apply

Choose a loan and go through a guided application flow in a few taps.

Track

See each application's progress on one status screen.
